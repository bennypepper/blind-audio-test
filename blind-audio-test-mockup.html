Blind Audio Test

Can you hear the difference?

Each song comes in four versions: one lossless, three compressed the way popular streaming services do it. Listen to all four, then pick the one you think sounds best.

On a phone? Speakers and Bluetooth earbuds can hide the differences. For a meaningful result, use a computer with wired headphones.
