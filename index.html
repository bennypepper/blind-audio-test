    Why rest your ears?

    Continuous critical listening causes high-frequency adaptation and temporary ear fatigue. Taking 30 seconds of quiet allows your ear's hair cells and brain to reset their transient sensitivity for the next track.

    ABX Instructions & Keyboard Shortcuts

    How the ABX Protocol Works

    1. In each trial, A is the lossless CD master, B is the lossy comparison format, and X is the secret target (randomly assigned to either A or B with 50/50 probability by cryptographic CSPRNG).

    2. Switch between A, X, and B seamlessly while listening. Playback synchronizes sample-accurately with a 10ms micro-crossfade to eliminate telltale switching clicks.

    3. Audition Engagement Gate: To prevent hasty guessing, you must listen to each source (A, X, and B) for at least 2.0 seconds before the choice buttons unlock.

    4. Once unlocked, decide whether X is A or X is B, then click Next (or press Enter) to advance.

    5. Trials are strictly double-blind: no immediate feedback is given during testing. Exact binomial p-values, 95% Clopper–Pearson confidence intervals, and Holm–Bonferroni corrections are displayed upon completion.
