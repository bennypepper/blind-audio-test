Blind Audio Test

Can you hear the difference?

Each song comes in four versions: one lossless, three compressed the way popular streaming services do it. Switch between them while the song plays and pick the one with the highest quality.

On a phone? Speakers and Bluetooth earbuds can hide acoustic differences. For a meaningful test, we recommend a computer with wired headphones.
